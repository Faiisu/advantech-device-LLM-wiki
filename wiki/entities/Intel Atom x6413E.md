# Intel Atom x6413E

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Elkhart Lake  
**Released:** 2021

## Overview

Quad-core Intel Atom SoC from the Elkhart Lake platform — Intel's 2021 embedded refresh. Supports DDR4 3200 MHz (vs DDR3L in Apollo Lake), Intel UHD Graphics (Gen11), and is designed for extended temperature operation down to -40°C. Also available as Intel Celeron J6412 (same platform, different SKU).

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.5 GHz
- Memory: DDR4 3200 MHz, up to 32 GB (dual channel SO-DIMM)
- TDP: ~9–12W — fanless capable
- Operating temp: down to -40°C in ARK-1221L
- Platform also includes: Intel Celeron J6412 (4c/1.5GHz), Celeron N6210 (2c/1.2GHz) on same chipset
