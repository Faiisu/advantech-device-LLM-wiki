# Advantech

**Type:** Organization (Hardware Manufacturer)  
**Headquarters:** Taiwan  
**Website:** advantech.com

## Overview

Taiwanese industrial computing company specializing in embedded PCs, industrial automation hardware, IoT gateways, and edge computing platforms. One of the leading vendors in the industrial PC (IPC) market globally. Known for long product lifecycles, wide temperature range support, and modular expansion (iDoor). Software ecosystem includes SUSIAccess and DeviceOn (WISE-PaaS).
