# Intel Core i5-8365UE

**Type:** Product (Processor)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Whiskey Lake-U (8th Gen Core, embedded UE variant)  
**Released:** 2018–2019

## Overview

Quad-core 8th Gen Intel Core i5, UE-variant (ultra-low voltage, embedded). The Whiskey Lake generation predates Tiger Lake but offers higher burst frequency (4.1 GHz) than the Tiger Lake i5-1145G7E's burst (unspecified in source). Intel UHD Graphics 620.

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.6 GHz, burst **4.1 GHz**
- Memory: DDR4 2400 MHz, up to 32 GB
- GPU: Intel UHD Graphics 620
- TDP: 15W
- Typical system power: 13.87W (ARK-1551), max 47.12W

## Role in This Wiki

Used in [[ARK-1551]] — slim fanless wall-mount PC with swappable drive bay, USB 3.1 Gen2, RED certified.
