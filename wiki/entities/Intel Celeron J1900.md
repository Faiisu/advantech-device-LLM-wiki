# Intel Celeron J1900

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Bay Trail-D (Atom-based SoC)  
**Released:** ~2013–2014

## Overview

Intel's quad-core Celeron SoC from the Bay Trail-D platform. Designed for embedded and low-power applications. Integrates CPU, GPU, memory controller, and I/O on a single chip. Widely used in industrial PCs, thin clients, and entry-level embedded systems.

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, 4 threads (no hyperthreading beyond 1:1)
- Base: 2.0 GHz, Burst: 2.41 GHz
- L2 Cache: 2 MB
- Integrated Intel Gen 7 Graphics (688 MHz)
- TDP: ~10W (low-power SoC — enables fanless designs)
- Memory: Dual-channel DDR3L 1333 MHz

## Role in This Wiki

The processor used in the [[ARK-6322]]. Its low TDP is what enables fanless operation in that platform.
