# Intel N200

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Alder Lake-N (Intel Processor N-series)  
**Released:** 2023

## Overview

Quad-core Intel Processor from the Alder Lake-N platform. The N-series is Intel's latest embedded/IoT SoC generation (2023), supporting DDR5-4800, significantly higher burst frequencies than Apollo Lake, and improved GPU (Intel UHD Graphics). Higher-performance bin than the x7211E within Alder Lake-N.

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.0 GHz, burst **3.7 GHz** (much higher than E3940's 1.8 GHz burst)
- Memory: DDR5 up to 4800 MHz, up to 16 GB
- GPU: Intel UHD Graphics (Alder Lake-N integrated)
- TDP: ~6W base — fanless capable despite high burst frequency

## Role in This Wiki

Used in [[ARK-1125H]] — dual CAN Bus, dual 2.5GbE, dual HDMI 4K, RED certified.
