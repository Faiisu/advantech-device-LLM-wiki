# Intel 8th-9th Gen Core (LGA1151)

**Type:** Processor
**Also known as:** Coffee Lake (8th Gen), Coffee Lake Refresh (9th Gen); LGA1151 v2 socket; desktop/embedded socketed platform

## Overview

Intel 8th and 9th Generation Core processors use the LGA1151 socket (v2, incompatible with 6th/7th Gen LGA1151 boards) with Coffee Lake microarchitecture. The platform supports DDR4 2400/2666MHz dual-channel memory and up to 8 cores / 16 threads. Chipsets include H310 (value), Q370 (business/vPro), and Z390 (enthusiast).

## Key Facts

- Socket: LGA1151 (v2 — physically same but electrically incompatible with 6th/7th Gen LGA1151)
- TDP: up to 65W (desktop 65W-class)
- Cores: up to 8 (i7/i9 class), 4–6 for i3–i5
- L3 Cache: up to 12MB (8-core i7)
- Memory: Dual-channel DDR4 2400/2666MHz, up to 64GB (2× SO-DIMM in embedded)
- Chipsets used in MIC-770: Intel H310 (consumer), Intel Q370 (business/vPro)
- Intel I219-V (consumer NIC, H310) vs Intel I219-LM (AMT-capable, Q370)

## Role in This Wiki

Used in the [[MIC-770]] (V1), the direct successor to the MIC-7700's 6th/7th Gen platform. The Q370 chipset variant enables vPro / AMT and RAID support, while H310 is a cost-optimised option.
