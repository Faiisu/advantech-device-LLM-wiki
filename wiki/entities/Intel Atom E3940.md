# Intel Atom E3940

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Apollo Lake (E3x00 embedded series)  
**Released:** 2016–2017

## Overview

Quad-core Intel Atom SoC from Apollo Lake, the embedded-grade variant (E-series) of the same platform as the N-series Celeron. Designed for extended lifecycle support and wider temperature operation. Intel HD Graphics 500 (Gen9 LP). Used across multiple ARK-1000/1200 series models.

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.6 GHz, burst 1.8 GHz
- L2 Cache: 2 MB
- Memory: DDR3L 1600–1866 MHz (up to 8 GB)
- GPU: Intel HD Graphics 500, Gen9 LP; H.265/HEVC, DirectX 12, OpenCL 2.0
- TDP: ~9.5W — fanless capable

## Role in This Wiki

Used in three distinct ARK models with different feature focuses:
- [[ARK-1124H]]: dual HDMI, TPM, iDoor
- [[ARK-1220F]]: isolated I/O (2.5 kV), DIN-rail
- [[ARK-1220L]]: standard DIN-rail, -30–70°C
