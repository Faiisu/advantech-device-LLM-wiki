# Intel Core i5-1145G7E

**Type:** Product (Processor)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Tiger Lake-UP3 (11th Gen Core, embedded variant)  
**Released:** 2021

## Overview

Quad-core 11th Gen Intel Core i5, embedded-grade variant (E-suffix = extended temperature and lifecycle). Part of the Tiger Lake platform. Significantly higher performance than Atom/Celeron SoCs — supports DDR4 up to 64 GB, much higher IPC, and Intel Iris Xe Graphics. Paired with i3-1115G4E (dual-core) in the ARK-1250L.

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.5 GHz, burst higher (not specified in source)
- Memory: DDR4 3200 MHz, up to 64 GB (dual SO-DIMM)
- GPU: Intel Iris Xe / UHD Graphics
- TDP: 12–15W (configurable; higher TDP than Atom/Celeron SoCs)
- Typical system power: 19.8W (ARK-1250L), max 35.1W

## Role in This Wiki

Highest-performance CPU in this wiki's ARK series. Used in [[ARK-1250L]] — triple GbE, 4× COM, -40–60°C, 64 GB RAM.
