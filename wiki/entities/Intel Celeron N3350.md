# Intel Celeron N3350

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Apollo Lake  
**Released:** 2016

## Overview

Dual-core Intel Celeron SoC from the Apollo Lake platform. Successor to Bay Trail (J1900). Integrates Intel HD Graphics 500 (Gen9 LP, DirectX 12). Used in low-power embedded and fanless systems requiring modest performance.

## Key Specs

- 2 cores, base 1.1 GHz, burst 2.4 GHz
- L2 Cache: 2 MB
- Memory: DDR3L 1866 MHz (up to 8 GB in ARK implementations)
- GPU: Intel HD Graphics 500, Gen9 LP, H.265/HEVC decode/encode, DirectX 12
- TDP: ~6W — enables fanless operation

## Role in This Wiki

Used in [[ARK-11]] (DIN-rail, -30–70°C) and [[ARK-1124C]] (DIN-rail, 4× COM). Same platform generation as [[Intel Atom E3940]] (quad-core Apollo Lake sibling).
