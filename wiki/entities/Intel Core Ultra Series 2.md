# Intel Core Ultra Series 2

**Type:** Processor
**Also known as:** Intel Core Ultra 200 series, Meteor Lake-S (desktop), Arrow Lake-S; socket LGA1851

## Overview

Intel Core Ultra Processors (Series 2) are Intel's next-generation desktop/embedded processors featuring an integrated NPU (Neural Processing Unit) for on-device AI inference, Intel Xe LPG graphics, and support for DDR5 memory up to 6400MHz. The LGA1851 socket replaces LGA1700 for this generation.

## Key Facts

- Socket: LGA1851
- TDP: up to 65W (configurable)
- Cores: up to 24 (P-cores + E-cores + LP-E-cores)
- L3 Cache: up to 36MB
- Memory: Dual-channel DDR5 up to 6400MHz, 128GB max
- Graphics: Intel Xe LPG integrated GPU
- NPU: integrated Neural Processing Unit (enables local AI inference without discrete GPU)
- Chipsets in MIC-780: Intel W880 (enterprise/vPro) and Intel H810 (mainstream)

## Role in This Wiki

The Intel Core Ultra Series 2 is the most recent Intel processor generation represented in this wiki. It introduces the NPU — a dedicated AI accelerator — to the fanless industrial box PC category via the [[MIC-780]], which Advantech markets as the first fanless industrial box PC with integrated NPU.
