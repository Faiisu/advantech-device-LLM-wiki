# Intel Atom x7211E

**Type:** Product (Processor / SoC)  
**Manufacturer:** Intel  
**Family:** Alder Lake-N  
**Released:** 2023

## Overview

Quad-core Intel Atom SoC from Alder Lake-N platform. The embedded-grade (E-suffix) variant designed for extended lifecycle and temperature requirements. Supports DDR5-4800. Lower-performance bin than Intel N200 within the same platform (3.2 GHz burst vs 3.7 GHz).

## Key Specs

- 4 cores, base 1.0 GHz, burst 3.2 GHz
- Memory: DDR5 up to 4800 MHz, up to 16 GB
- TDP: low — fanless capable
- IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 certified in ARK-1125C implementation

## Role in This Wiki

Used in [[ARK-1125C]] — 4× COM, DDR5, 3× M.2, Ubuntu 22.04, IEC 62443-4-2 certified.
