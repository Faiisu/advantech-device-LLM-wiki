# IEC 62443

## Definition

An international series of standards (IEC 62443) defining requirements and processes for securing Industrial Automation and Control Systems (IACS). Covers security at the component, system integrator, and asset owner levels. The relevant part for embedded hardware is **IEC 62443-4-2**, which defines technical security requirements for IACS components.

## Why It Matters

As OT (Operational Technology) systems become network-connected, they face cybersecurity threats traditionally associated with IT systems. IEC 62443 is the dominant OT security framework, increasingly required in critical infrastructure, manufacturing, and EU regulatory contexts (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Security Levels

| SL | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SL 1 | Protection against casual/unintentional violations |
| SL 2 | Protection against intentional violation with simple means |
| SL 3 | Protection against intentional violation with sophisticated means |
| SL 4 | State-sponsored/highly sophisticated attacks |
