# DIN-Rail Mounting

## Definition

A standardized metal rail (35 mm wide, per IEC/EN 60715) used to mount industrial equipment — PLCs, relays, power supplies, and embedded PCs — inside electrical control panels and cabinets. Devices clip onto the rail via a spring-loaded bracket. Standard in European industrial automation.

## Why It Matters

DIN-rail mounting is the dominant physical integration standard in industrial control panels. A DIN-rail-compatible embedded PC can sit alongside PLCs, I/O modules, and power supplies in the same panel, minimizing cabinet space and wiring runs. This is a key differentiator for ARK-1000/1200 series vs. the ARK-6322 (which targets wall/VESA/surface mounting).

## Key Properties

- **Rail standard:** 35 mm top-hat rail (EN 60715 TS35)
- **Clip mechanism:** spring-loaded snap-in; tool-free install/removal
- **I/O orientation:** DIN-rail-optimized devices put connectors on the **front bezel** (facing the panel door) rather than the rear — seen in ARK-11, ARK-1220L, etc.
- **Form factor impact:** DIN-rail devices are typically narrow and tall (e.g., 53.5 × 158 mm or 60 × 158 mm profile) rather than wide and flat
