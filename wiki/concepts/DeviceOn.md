# DeviceOn

## Definition

Advantech's IoT device management platform for remote monitoring, configuration, and management of embedded and edge computing devices. Formerly marketed as WISE-PaaS/DeviceOn; current branding is simply DeviceOn. Enables mass deployment, OTA updates, remote diagnostics, and AI application lifecycle management.

## Why It Matters

For deployments with many edge nodes (factory floors, retail chains, smart city), centralized device management reduces field service costs. DeviceOn provides a single pane of glass for Advantech hardware.

## Key Capabilities (from product mentions)

- Remote device monitoring and health checks
- OTA firmware/software updates
- AI model deployment at scale (mentioned in ARK-1250L context)
- Anomaly detection and alerting
- Integration with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, etc.)
