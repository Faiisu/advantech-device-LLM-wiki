# ARK-1123x Sub-series

**Part of:** [[Advantech]] ARK-1000 Series  
**Type:** Sub-series Overview + Selection Guide  
**Members:** [[ARK-1123H]], [[ARK-1123C]], [[ARK-1123L]]

## Overview

Three compact fanless box PCs sharing the same 133.8×43.1×94.2mm chassis. The "1123" form factor is the smallest in the ARK-1000 line. Models differ by CPU generation, display output, GbE count, GPIO presence, and EMC class. All use 12V DC power and a single SO-DIMM slot (max 8 GB DDR3L).

## Shared Characteristics

- Form factor: 133.8 × 43.1 × 94.2 mm (all three are identical)
- Power: 12 VDC, threaded DC jack, 36W adapter
- Storage: 1× 2.5" SATA HDD bay + 1× half-size mSATA (except ARK-1123L adds 2nd PCIe slot)
- Max RAM: 8 GB via 1× 204-pin SO-DIMM
- OS: Windows 10 / Linux

---

## Key Distinctions to Remember

- **ARK-1123H** is the only one with HDMI and RED certification path — display-centric or wireless use cases.
- **ARK-1123C vs ARK-1123L:** Identical CPU/temp/power. C has dual GbE + no GPIO (Class B). L has single GbE + GPIO (Class A). Choosing between them is purely about whether you need GPIO vs. second LAN.
- **Temperature paradox:** E3825 in ARK-1123C/L achieves -30~70°C; quad-core J1900 in ARK-1123H only reaches -20~60°C — lower TDP enables wider thermal range.

## Gaps and Open Questions

- Why does ARK-1123L's GPIO cause FCC Class A (not Class B)? Likely higher digital noise from GPIO lines — unconfirmed.
- SUSIAccess (ARK-1123H) vs. WISE-DeviceOn (ARK-1123C/L) — same platform generation? Possibly different firmware stacks for different product vintages.
