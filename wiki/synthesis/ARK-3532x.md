# ARK-3532x Sub-series Hub

**Part of:** [[Advantech]] ARK-3000 Series  
**Members:** [[ARK-3532B]], [[ARK-3532C]]  
**Prompted by:** Batch ingest of ARK-3000 series.  
**Type:** Comparison + Selection Guide

## Overview

Both models share the same 10th Gen Intel Xeon W / Core i LGA1200 platform (W480E chipset), DDR4 ECC 64GB, 4× GbE, 6× COM, 8× USB, and 16-bit GPIO. The decisive split is in the PCIe expansion tier: ARK-3532B provides a PCIe x16 graphics card slot (discrete GPU) while ARK-3532C replaces it with 2× PCI legacy slots.

## Selection Guide

| If you need… | Choose |
|---|---|

## Shared Characteristics

- Same Comet Lake-S/W LGA1200 CPU range (Celeron to 10-core Xeon W)
- ECC DDR4 support on both (by processor)
- 4× GbE — highest multi-port connectivity in the ARK-3000 series at this generation
- Wide-range 9~36 VDC input — compatible with 12V and 24V industrial power rails
- -20~60°C operating temperature (SSD); 0~40°C with HDD
- Fanless passive cooling despite 65W CPU TDP — requires 0.7 m/s airflow

---

## Key Distinctions

> **PCIe x16 (GPU) vs PCI (legacy):** This is the primary hardware selection axis. ARK-3532B adds a full-length PCIe x16 slot for high-bandwidth add-in cards (GPU, FPGA, 100GbE NIC). ARK-3532C trades that for 2× PCI — the right choice only if the deployment has existing PCI-based I/O cards that cannot be replaced.

> **TPM and SW bundle on B only:** If security attestation (TPM 2.0) or pre-integrated DeviceOn/McAfee management is a requirement, ARK-3532B is the only option.

> **ARK-3532C certification gap:** No EMC/safety certifications are listed in the ARK-3532C spec sheet. This may be an oversight or may indicate the model had not completed certification at the time of documentation.
