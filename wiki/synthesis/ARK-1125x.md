# ARK-1125x Sub-series

**Part of:** [[Advantech]] ARK-1000 Series  
**Type:** Sub-series Overview + Selection Guide  
**Members:** [[ARK-1125C]], [[ARK-1125H]]

## Overview

Two compact DIN-rail fanless box PCs on Intel Alder Lake-N (2023) — the newest generation in the ARK-1000 line. Both share the same 133×46.4×94.2mm chassis and DDR5-4800 memory. They differ at the use-case level: ARK-1125C targets cybersecurity-certified serial deployments; ARK-1125H targets CAN Bus and wireless-capable installations.

## Shared Characteristics

- Form factor: 133 × 46.4 × 94.2 mm (both identical)
- Platform: Intel Alder Lake-N (2023 generation)
- Memory: DDR5-4800, up to 16 GB, 1× 262-pin SO-DIMM
- GPIO: 8-bit on both
- TPM 2.0 built-in on both
- Operating temp: -30–60°C
- Power: 12 VDC, 60W adapter
- DIN-rail / Wall mounting
- Ubuntu 22.04 officially supported on both

---

## Key Distinctions to Remember

- **ARK-1125C** is the **cybersecurity model** — IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 is its headline feature. Pick it for OT/ICS deployments requiring formal certification.
- **ARK-1125H** is the **CAN + wireless model** — 2× CAN Bus (unique in compact tier) + RED certification. Pick it for vehicular, automotive, or CAN-network use cases.
- Both are a full generation leap over ARK-1124x: DDR5 vs DDR3L, 2.5GbE vs 1GbE, TPM 2.0 standard (not optional).
- **COM trade-off:** ARK-1125C doubles COM ports (4×) vs ARK-1125H (2×) — the latter gives up 2 COM ports in exchange for 2 CAN Bus ports.
