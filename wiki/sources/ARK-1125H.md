# ARK-1125H

**Type:** Product Specification Sheet  
**Author(s):** Advantech  
**Origin:** `raw/ARK-1125H.md` | https://www.advantech.com/th-th/products/1-2jkbyz/ark-1125h/  
**Sub-series:** [[ARK-1125x]]

## Summary

Compact fanless box PC on Intel Processor N200 (Alder Lake-N, quad-core). Key differentiators: **dual CAN Bus ports** (unique in the compact tier), **dual 2.5GbE** (Intel i226), DDR5-4800, dual HDMI 4K, TPM 2.0 built-in, and **RED certified** with selected wireless modules. Targets vehicular, automation, and CAN-network deployments.

## Key Specs

- **CPU:** Intel Processor N200, 4-core, 1.0 GHz (burst 3.7 GHz); Alder Lake-N
- **Memory:** DDR5 up to 4800 MHz, up to **16 GB**, 1× 262-pin SO-DIMM
- **Display:** 2× HDMI (both 4K2K); no VGA
- **Ethernet:** 2× Intel i226 **2.5GbE** (Wake on LAN)
- **Serial:** 2× RS-232/422/485 with auto flow control
- **CAN Bus:** **2× CAN Bus** (built-in)
- **USB:** 2× USB 3.2 + 2× USB 2.0; remote switch
- **GPIO:** 8-bit
- **Storage:** 2× M.2 (E key 2230, B key 2280 with nano SIM)
- **Security:** TPM 2.0 built-in
- **Power:** 12 VDC; 60W adapter; typical **10.54W**, max 29.18W
- **Operating temp:** -30–60°C
- **Dimensions:** 133 × 46.4 × 94.2 mm; 0.7 kg
- **Mounting:** DIN-rail / Wall
- **OS:** Windows 10; Ubuntu 22.04
- **Certifications:** RED certified with selected wireless modules

## Differentiators vs. ARK Series

- **2× CAN Bus** — enables direct connection to automotive/industrial CAN networks without adapter
- **Dual 2.5GbE** — highest ethernet bandwidth in compact tier
- **RED certified** — wireless-enabled deployments allowed in EU without additional certification
- Higher max power (29.18W vs 19.72W for ARK-1125C) — higher-performance N200 vs x7211E
