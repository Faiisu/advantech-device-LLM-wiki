# ARK-1220L

**Type:** Product Specification Sheet  
**Author(s):** Advantech  
**Origin:** `raw/ARK-1220L.md` | https://www.advantech.com/th-th/products/1-2jkbyz/ark-1220l/

## Summary

DIN-rail fanless box PC on Intel Atom E3940 (Apollo Lake). The "standard" display-capable DIN-rail model — dual HDMI 4K, dual GbE, 4× USB 3.0, 2× RS-232/422/485, GPIO, 12–28V wide-range power, and optional WISE-PaaS support. -30–70°C temperature range (note: **turbo burst must be disabled** to achieve -30°C).

## Key Specs

- **CPU:** Intel Atom E3940, 4-core, 1.6 GHz (burst 1.8 GHz); Apollo Lake
- **Memory:** DDR3L 1600 MHz, up to 8 GB, 2× SO-DIMM
- **Display:** 2× HDMI (4K); dual display
- **Ethernet:** 2× Intel i210 GbE (Wake on LAN)
- **Serial:** 2× RS-232/422/485 with auto flow control; COM2 with 5V/12V power supply
- **USB:** 4× USB 3.0
- **GPIO:** 8-bit
- **Storage:** 1× 2.5" SATAIII Drive bay (15mm compatible); 1× full-size mSATA (shares mPCIe slot)
- **Expansion:** 1× full-size mPCIe with SIM holder; 1× M.2 2230 (WiFi)
- **Security:** TPM by project (not standard)
- **Power:** 12–28 VDC wide range; 60W adapter; typical **7.6W**, max 19W
- **Operating temp:** -30–70°C (turbo off, extended peripherals); 0–40°C with HDD
- **Dimensions:** 53.5 × 158 × 114 mm; 0.9 kg
- **Mounting:** DIN-rail / Wall
- **OS:** Windows 10; Ubuntu (supported)
- **Certifications:** no RED (certifications not listed in source)

## Differentiators vs. ARK Series

- **-30–70°C** temperature range — tied with ARK-11 for widest; requires turbo burst disabled below -30°C
- Dual HDMI 4K in slim DIN form (53.5 mm wide)
- COM2 carries 5V/12V power supply — can power external RS-232 devices directly
- WISE-PaaS / Advantech IoT platform support
- TPM only by project (not standard) — less secure than ARK-1124H out of box
