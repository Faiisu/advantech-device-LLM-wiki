# ARK-1125C

**Type:** Product Specification Sheet  
**Author(s):** Advantech  
**Origin:** `raw/ARK-1125C.md` | https://www.advantech.com/th-th/products/1-2jkbyz/ark-1125c/  
**Sub-series:** [[ARK-1125x]]

## Summary

Modern-generation fanless box PC on Intel Atom x7211E (Alder Lake-N platform, quad-core). Major generational leap: **DDR5-4800 memory (up to 16 GB)**, 3× M.2 slots (E/M/B keys), TPM 2.0 built-in, Ubuntu 22.04 officially supported, and IEC 62443-4-2 Security Level 2 certified — making this the first ARK in this wiki with formal cybersecurity certification. Serial-focused (4× COM) in a compact form.

## Key Specs

- **CPU:** Intel Atom x7211E, 4-core, 1.0 GHz (burst 3.2 GHz); Alder Lake-N
- **Memory:** DDR5 up to 4800 MHz, up to **16 GB**, 1× 262-pin SO-DIMM
- **Display:** 1× HDMI
- **Ethernet:** 1× Intel i226 2.5GbE (Wake on LAN)
- **Serial:** **4× RS-232/422/485**
- **USB:** 2× USB 3.2 + 2× USB 2.0; remote switch support
- **GPIO:** 8-bit
- **Storage:** 3× M.2 (E key PCIe x1, M key PCIe x1/SATA, B key PCIe x1 with nano SIM); one SKU has 128 GB NVMe built-in
- **Security:** TPM 2.0 built-in (not optional)
- **Power:** 12 VDC; 60W adapter; typical **10.05W**, max 19.72W
- **Operating temp:** -30–60°C
- **Dimensions:** 133 × 46.4 × 94.2 mm; 0.7 kg
- **Mounting:** DIN-rail / Wall
- **OS:** Windows 10/11 IoT Enterprise LTSC; Ubuntu 22.04 (officially supported)
- **Certifications:** IEC 62443-4-2 (Security Level 2); Advantech ECO Gold

## Differentiators vs. ARK Series

- **DDR5** — generational jump from DDR3L in older ARK models
- **IEC 62443-4-2 SL2** — first in this wiki with OT/industrial cybersecurity certification
- **2.5GbE** (Intel i226) vs 1GbE in older models
- **Ubuntu 22.04 officially supported** — not "by project" — signals Linux-first positioning
- 3× M.2 slots — most flexible storage/wireless configuration in compact tier
- EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance readiness noted
