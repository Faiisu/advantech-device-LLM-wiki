# ARK-1220F

**Type:** Product Specification Sheet  
**Author(s):** Advantech  
**Origin:** `raw/ARK-1220F.md` | https://www.advantech.com/th-th/products/1-2jkbyz/ark-1220f/

## Summary

DIN-rail fanless box PC on Intel Atom E3940 with the standout feature of **2.5 kV DC galvanic isolation on both GbE ports, both COM ports, and the 8-bit GPIO** — the only model in this series with hardware-level electrical isolation. Targets deployments with high electrical noise or ground potential differences (industrial machinery, power systems, harsh field environments). Also has dual display (HDMI + VGA), 12–28V wide-range input, TPM 2.0, and 4× USB 3.0.

## Key Specs

- **CPU:** Intel Atom E3940, 4-core, 1.6 GHz (burst 1.8 GHz); Apollo Lake
- **Memory:** DDR3L 1600 MHz, up to 8 GB, 2× SO-DIMM
- **Display:** 1× HDMI (4K) + 1× VGA; dual display (HDMI+VGA standard, dual HDMI by project)
- **Ethernet:** 2× Intel i210-IT GbE — **2.5 kV DC isolated**
- **Serial:** 1× RS-232/422/485 (default RS-485) + 1× RS-422/485 (default RS-485) — **both 2.5 kV DC isolated**
- **USB:** 4× USB 3.0 + 1× USB 2.0 (not isolated)
- **GPIO:** 8-bit — **2.5 kV DC isolated** (4DI/4DO)
- **Storage:** 1× 2.5" SATAIII drive bay (15mm compatible); 1× full-size mSATA (shares mPCIe slot)
- **Expansion:** 1× full-size mPCIe with nano SIM holder; 1× M.2 2230 (WiFi)
- **Security:** TPM 2.0 (Infineon SLB9665XQ)
- **Power:** 12–28 VDC wide range; typical power TBD
- **Operating temp:** -30–60°C (extended); 0–40°C with HDD
- **Dimensions:** 60 × 158 × 114 mm; 1.5 kg
- **Mounting:** DIN-rail / Wall
- **OS:** Windows 10; Linux by project
- **Certifications:** CE/FCC Class B; UL/CB/CCC/BSMI; **no RED**

## Differentiators vs. ARK Series

- **Galvanic isolation (2.5 kV)** on GbE, COM, and GPIO — unique in this entire series
- Wider DIN form factor (60 mm wide vs 53.5 mm for ARK-11, ARK-1220L) to accommodate isolation circuitry
- HDMI + VGA dual display (vs dual HDMI in ARK-1220L) — wider legacy monitor compatibility
- Only 2× COM vs 4× in ARK-1124C — isolation is the trade-off
