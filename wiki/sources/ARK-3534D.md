# ARK-3534D

**Type:** Product Specification Sheet  
**Author(s):** Advantech  
**Date:** N/A  
**Origin:** `raw/ark-3000series/ARK-3534D.md` | https://www.advantech.com/th-th/products/1-2jkd2d/ark-3534d/
**Sub-series:** [[ARK-3534x]]

## Summary

The ARK-3534D is the higher-spec variant of the ARK-3534 sub-series. Same 12th/13th/14th Gen LGA1700 platform as the ARK-3534C but with Intel R680E chipset, ECC DDR5 support, 4× GbE (vs 2×), PCIe x4 slot, and Intel SW RAID. Also carries IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 and 2× CAN Bus. Targets mission-critical deployments requiring ECC memory integrity, redundant storage, and multi-network connectivity alongside high-performance PCIe expansion.

## Key Points

- **CPU:** Intel 12/13/14th Gen 65W Embedded Series; LGA1700; Intel **R680E** chipset
- **Memory:** DDR5 4800 MHz, up to 64 GB, 2× 262-pin SO-DIMM; **ECC supported** (by processor)
- **Display:** 2× HDMI + optional 3rd (no VGA)
- **LAN:** 4× — Intel I219-LM GbE + **3× Intel i225 2.5GbE** (vs 2× GbE on ARK-3534C)
- **COM:** 4× RS-232/422/485 (base) + optional COM7/8 = up to 6 total
- **USB:** 8× USB 3.0
- **CAN Bus:** 2×
- **GPIO:** 16-bit DIO (feature list)
- **TPM:** 2.0
- **Expansion:** 1× M.2 B-key (2242/3042) + 1× M.2 E-key (2230), **1× PCIe x4**, 2× PCI, 1× PCIe x16
- **Storage:** 2× 2.5" SATAIII (up to 3×); Intel SW RAID supported
- **Construction:** Fanless; sheet metal chassis with aluminum top cover
- **Power:** AT/ATX, 9~36 VDC; power consumption not listed
- **Temp:** Not listed in spec sheet
- **OS:** Windows 10/11 IoT Enterprise LTSC
- **Cert:** CE/FCC Class B, CCC, BSMI, UKCA; CB, UL, CCC, BSMI, UKCA
- **IEC 62443-4-2 SL2** conformity; supports EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance
- **SW:** SUSI API, WISE-DeviceOn, McAfee, Acronis bundled
