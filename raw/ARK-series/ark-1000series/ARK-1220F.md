Intel Atom E3940 QC SoC with isolated GbE, COM and DIO ports DIN-Rail Fanless Box PC

- Intel Atom® E3940 Quad Core SoC turbo burst up to 1.8 GHz
- DIN-Rail system with essential I/O ports on front-side bezel
- Isolated Intel GbE and COM with protection to 2.5K VDC
- Isolated 8 bit GPIO (4DI/4DO) with with protection to 2.5K VDC
- 12V ~ 28V wide range power input
- Dual independent displays with one 4K HDMI and one VGA
- 1 x full-size mPCIe with nano SIM holder and 1 x M.2 2230 for WIFI
- mSATA and 1 x 2.5" SATA storage device
- No RED Certification
